# Contributing

Extended Korean (EK) aims to write every language's sounds in one Hangul-based script.
It needs linguists, native speakers, type designers and engineers from every language
community. The current focus is **English (including American dialects), Spanish and
Korean**.

## Before your first contribution

1. Read [LICENSES.md](LICENSES.md). The writing system, word data and fonts are open;
   the code and models are free for noncommercial use and licensed to companies.
2. Sign the [Contributor License Agreement](CLA.md). The CLA bot asks you on your first
   pull request.

## Ways to contribute

### Fix how a word is written

1. Open the [word editor](editor/), or a Synced Lyrics example under
   [examples/](examples/).
2. In the editor, type the spelling you hear, in standard Hangul or block notation like
   `[ㅋ+ㅓ+ㄹ]`, then press **Export**. On an example page, use **✎ Review**: click a
   word while it plays, write what you hear, then export.
3. Open an issue, attach the exported JSON, and say which language and dialect you
   speak.

Maintainers merge accepted spellings into the project lexicon and regenerate the pages.

### Record your dialect

We are collecting the same short paragraph from speakers of many dialects, starting with
Texas, other US regions, Spanish varieties, and Korean 경상·전라·충청·강원·제주 speech.
Open an issue titled **"Recording: <your dialect>"**, and we will send you the paragraph
and the consent form. Never upload anyone else's voice without their written consent.

### Become a language linguist

Each language has a lead linguist who approves changes to that language's spellings.
Paid stipends are funded by the project's sponsors. Open an issue titled
**"Linguist: <language>"** with your languages, your phonetics background, and an IPA
sample of five words.

### Type design

The EK font is built on Noto Sans KR under the SIL Open Font License 1.1. Glyph
feedback is welcome in issues; please include a screenshot.
